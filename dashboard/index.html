How the value is worked out

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × span

Growth follows the protocol's own 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50…+100%. Maturity follows how long it has run with no token recorded, up to 4 years. Span follows how many chains it runs on. No name is ever special-cased — the same formula scores every row. Move the sliders and the whole board reprices live.

A note on what “no token” means. A protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is what the record says, never proof it has no token. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out: they are not launches waiting to happen.
